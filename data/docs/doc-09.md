# Brandhuis Koffie — coffee contract Café Kiem

Coffee contract between Brandhuis Koffie and Café Kiem, 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2027.

- Brandhuis provides an espresso machine on loan and services it twice a year.
- Café Kiem buys all its espresso beans from Brandhuis, at least 6 kg per week on average.
- Fixed prices for the duration of the contract: house blend EUR 24.00/kg, decaf EUR 26.50/kg, milk-system cleaner EUR 14.50 per bottle.
- Delivery on Monday; orders by email before Friday 12:00.
