# Bakkerij De Korenbloem — ordering rules

- Daily delivery at 07:00, Monday to Saturday. Order by WhatsApp before 14:00 the day before.
- Croissants EUR 0.95, sourdough loaf EUR 4.20, apple pie (12 slices) EUR 19.50, banana bread EUR 9.80.
- Missing items are credited on the next invoice; report them the same morning.
- Closed 25–26 December and 1 January. Orders for 24 and 31 December before 12:00 two days earlier.
