# Slagerij Hendriks — price agreement Bistro Vonk

Price agreement between Slagerij Hendriks and Bistro Vonk, signed 1 October 2026.

- Fixed prices until 31 March 2027: beef rib-eye EUR 32.50/kg, chicken thighs EUR 8.90/kg, minced beef EUR 10.40/kg.
- Delivery on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Orders by WhatsApp or phone before 16:00 the day before.
- Missing or wrong items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery; Hendriks credits them on the next invoice.
- Bistro Vonk buys all its meat from Hendriks for the duration of this agreement.
