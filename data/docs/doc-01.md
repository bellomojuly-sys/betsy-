# Van Beek Groente & Fruit — agreement with Bistro Vonk

Van Beek Groente & Fruit has supplied Bistro Vonk since 2018. Agreement made in person between Mark de Vries and Peter van Beek.

- Van Beek is the first choice for all fresh produce of Bistro Vonk.
- Orders by WhatsApp before 20:00 for delivery the next morning, Tuesday to Saturday.
- Prices follow the weekly price list (see DOC-02). No minimum order.
- Wrong or damaged items: report them by WhatsApp on the day of delivery. They are picked up with the next delivery and credited on the next invoice.
- Van Beek does not supply dairy, meat or fish.
